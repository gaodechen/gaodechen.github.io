We present [VITA](https://ucd-dare.github.io/VITA) (**accepted to ICLR 2026**), vision-to-action flow matching policy, which evolves latent images, instead of random Gaussian, to actions. VITA is the first MLP-only policy that handles complex real-world bimanual tasks such as ALOHA.
