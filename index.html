Dechen Gao

🎓 CS PhD Candidate | 🏫 UC Davis | 📨 dechengao at gmail dot com

Welcome! I am Dechen Gao, a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of California, Davis. I received my Master of Science in Computer Science from UC Davis in 2023, and later transferred to the CS PhD program with the support of my advisors Dr. Junshan Zhang and Dr. Iman Soltani. I am grateful to work with my advisors, and collaborate with all my labmates and DARE and LARA.

My research focuses on robot learning, particularly in reinforcement learning, imitation learning, world models, and foundation models. I am always open to discussions adn collaborations on these topics - feel free to drop me an email if you are interested!

I interned at Meta Reality Labs Research (2024), Meta Instagram(2022), TikTok (2024), and Amazon (2025).

News

Jul 01, 2025 We present VITA (accepted to ICLR 2026), vision-to-action flow matching policy, which evolves latent images, instead of random Gaussian, to actions. VITA is the first MLP-only policy that handles complex real-world bimanual tasks such as ALOHA.
May 01, 2025 Imitation or reinforcement policy fine-tuning? You may need both! Check out IN-RIL which interleaves between two objectives for better sample-efficiency and stability.
Sep 01, 2024 Are robots able to actively locate perspectives to solve tasks just like human do? Check out our ICRA work AV-ALOHA that applies active vision for bimanual manipulation!
May 01, 2024 We open-sourced CarDreamer , the first world model based learning platform for autonomous driving!
